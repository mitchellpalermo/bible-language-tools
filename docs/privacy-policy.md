# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 11, 2026**

This policy covers [greek.tools](https://greek.tools) and [hebrew.tools](https://hebrew.tools) (collectively, "the Sites"), operated by Mitchell Palermo.

## What we collect

**Account information**
When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We store these to identify your account.

**Study data**
We store your spaced repetition (SRS) card progress (scheduling intervals, due dates, review history), study statistics (streak, total reviews, accuracy), and any custom vocabulary decks you create. This data is linked to your account and synced across your devices.

**Session data**
We store a session token along with your IP address and browser/device type (user agent string) to keep you signed in. Sessions expire after 30 days.

**Analytics**
We use PostHog to collect page view counts, feature interaction events, and JavaScript error reports. PostHog records sessions for approximately 20% of visits to help us diagnose bugs. PostHog data is stored on servers in the United States.

**Browser storage**
Before you sign in, study data is saved in your browser's `localStorage` and stays on your device. It is only uploaded to our servers when you explicitly sync.

## How we use your data

- To identify your account and keep you signed in
- To sync your study progress across devices
- To maintain your study streak and statistics
- To diagnose errors and improve the app

## What we don't do

- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not use your data to serve advertisements
- We do not share your data with data brokers or advertising platforms

Your account and study data are retained for as long as your account is active. Session tokens expire after 30 days.
